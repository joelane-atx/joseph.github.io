Sue runs the sales org from one place: she sets the tasks, HubSpot confirms them

Everything opens from this folder, reads HubSpot only, sends nothing. The main build follows Sue's written brief of 2 Nov and the clarifications since: the tasks are Sue's, not HubSpot's; she has super control; everyone else gets their own view that she can manage from.

The platform · future style
Sales Control · the build

Sue's super-control view, and a view for everyone else

Sue: enters tasks (one person, a team, or all BDMs), accepts or dismisses suggestions from her own rules, reassigns, changes status, adds notes, asks for updates in Teams, logs replies, reads everyone's KPIs and activity, Friday report, forecast, win/loss, initiatives, announcements, coaching cockpit, audits, invoices, success measures.
Team Lead, John, Rep: the same platform in their own scope. A rep sees only their list, their numbers, Sue's asks, and can reply or mark blocked with a reason. Sue can "view as" any of them.

  • Tasks live in the agent; HubSpot is only the evidence line on each task
  • Teams messages are queued, not sent: needs IT consent, Joe pastes by hand until then
  • Reset demo clears what was entered in this browser
Open Sales Control · classicOpen Sales Control · orbit

Two versions of the same build to compare: classic opens on the tiles; orbit opens on a spinning 3D graph of the org (people as hubs, their accounts, deals and meetings around them) with a telemetry column. Both have the light and dark toggle, same data, same tasks.

Delivery

How a rep receives Sue's list

The same list four ways: in Claude, as a Teams post, as the Friday email, as a HubSpot task queue. The four build options from the brief, costed, with a suggested path.
