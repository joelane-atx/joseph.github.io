How the VAPI handoff works
After the 3-email sequence completes without a reply, the platform creates a HubSpot task ("Call ready") and fires a webhook to VAPI containing: contact name, title, company, a summary of the email thread, and a Claude-generated call script. VAPI queues the AI call automatically. When the call completes, the outcome is logged back to HubSpot and the deal stage is updated.
